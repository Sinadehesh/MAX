Process

Seven steps from CAD to finished mold.

Every project follows the same path, so you always know what happens next, what you'll receive and when it's your turn to decide.

  1. 01

    Project Intake

    Tell us about your part through a short form: geometry, materials, tools and constraints.

    OutputA clear brief we both agree on.

  2. 02

    Feasibility Audit Free

    We review your geometry and flag risks before you spend anything: manufacturability, lamination and demolding.

    OutputA risk report within 48 hours.

  3. 03

    FEA & Layup Optional

    We validate performance with structural analysis and define the ply schedule.

    OutputAnalysis report and ply schedule.

  4. 04

    Optimization & Mold Design

    We suggest modifications and set the technical direction, then design the mold around your process.

    OutputOptimized geometry and mold CAD.

  5. Approval gate: nothing is manufactured until you sign off.
  6. 05

    Review & Approval Your sign-off

    We go through a pre-delivery checklist together. You approve the design before any manufacturing starts, and changes are cheap because nothing has been built yet.

    OutputYour signed-off design.

  7. 06

    Manufacturing

    We build your mold and jigs to the approved design.

    OutputTooling, with inspection photos before shipping.

  8. 07

    Delivery & Priority Support

    You receive your tooling and files, with guidance and chat assistance while you start making parts.

    OutputMold, jigs, files and ongoing support.
