What we do

Three services, from analysis to finished tooling.

Book one on its own, or run your part through all three with a single team.

01

FEA & Layup Design

Structural Intelligence & Ply Scheduling

Stop guessing your laminate architecture or risking structural failure. We engineering-proof your composite parts to ensure performance right where it matters.

  • Material Optimization: The ideal fiber-to-resin ratio tailored to your target loads.
  • Weight Reduction: Every single ply is structurally justified to eliminate dead weight.
  • Certified Safety: FEA simulation data replacing high-stakes empirical guesswork.
Tick “FEA & Layup Design” in the form to optimize your part. Learn more about FEA & Layup Design
02

Mold Design

Precision Tooling Blueprinting

You have the part geometry and the shop capacity, but a bad mold layout will ruin your production. We design the tool so you can manufacture with confidence.

  • Flawless Demolding: Custom draft angles and parting lines optimized for your process.
  • Shrinkage Control: Advanced geometry corrections based on resin and curing behavior.
  • Production-Ready 3D: Blueprints tailored to Autoclave, Infusion, or Hand Lay-up.
Tick “Mold Design” in the form to design your mold. Learn more about Mold Design
03

Custom Tooling & Manufacturing

Turnkey Production Tools

Bypass internal bottlenecks and tight CNC tolerances. We manufacture and deliver the physical, custom production tools you need to streamline your laminating, trimming, and assembly phases.

  • Complete Production Kit: High-precision molds, assembly jigs, and custom trimming fixtures.
  • Tight CNC Tolerances: Flawless physical execution matching your exact digital geometry.
  • Plug-and-Play Integration: Fully finished tools delivered straight to your line, ready for use.
Tick “Mold & Tooling Manufacturing” in the form to receive your physical parts, ready to use. Learn more about Custom Tooling & Manufacturing
