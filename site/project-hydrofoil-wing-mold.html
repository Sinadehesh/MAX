The challenge

A hydrofoil wing combines a thin, sharp trailing edge, a curved planform and a surface finish that directly affects how it performs on the water. The mold has to split cleanly along the leading and trailing edges, keep both halves accurately aligned, and release the part without damaging those thin edges.
